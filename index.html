How to Play

Arrow Keys / WASD Move your character
SPACE / E Talk to NPCs & interact
Walk into enemies Start a math battle
I Open inventory
M Open map
1-4 Select answer in battle

Explore

Walk around villages, forests, caves, and castles. Talk to NPCs for clues and hints. Find treasure chests with secret rewards.

Fight

When you encounter an enemy, solve math problems to attack! Correct answers deal damage. Wrong answers let the enemy hit you. Chain correct answers for combo damage!

Progress

Earn gold and XP from battles. Buy items from the shop. Unlock new areas by defeating bosses. Find hidden secrets in every world!
